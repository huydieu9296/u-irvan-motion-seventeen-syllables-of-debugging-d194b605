Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Seventeen Syllables of Debugging"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-irvan-motion-seventeen-syllables-of-debugging-d194b605): one haiku about debugging, three lines in the 5-7-5 syllable shape, at most 200 characters, plain text.

## The haiku

> Logs scroll past midnight
> one lost semicolon hides
> turns out it was me

5-7-5 syllables, 17 in total, 70 characters.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the haiku to `/output/entry.txt`, after self-checking the
gate: exactly three lines, 1–200 characters, and the 5-7-5 syllable shape.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
